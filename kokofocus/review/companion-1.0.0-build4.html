Recording details

Recorded November 3, 2026 on a physical iPad Air (5th generation), iPadOS 27.0 (24A437), using Apple Pencil and finger input. The released Mac app is kokofocus 1.5.0 (19), running on macOS 27.2. The iPad footage is captured over USB; it is not a simulator. The Mac and iPad interact over the same private local network.

QR pairing, the corrected timer wheels, Stopwatch, Clock, finger clock gestures and disconnection are recorded on companion 1.0.0 (4). The clearly labeled drawing, shapes, effects, text and existing-permission sections were recorded on 1.0.0 (3). Those features are unchanged in build 4 and the footage remains valid. Playback stays at normal speed, with cuts between tasks.

The permission section shows existing Camera and Local Network access on iPad, and the Mac app’s Screen Recording access. Permissions were already granted; no switches were changed. The recorded QR code is no longer valid. Only fictional presentation content is mirrored.

The compatible, already released Mac app may show legacy “(beta)” wording in its Draw with iPad command. The production iPad companion has no beta-testing features or labels, tester enrollment, feedback collection or trial expiry.

Chapters

No account or passcode is required. Install kokofocus for Mac, connect both devices to the same private local network, choose Draw with iPad on Mac, and scan its QR code in the companion. A compatible Apple Pencil is required for drawing.
