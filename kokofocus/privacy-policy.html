Privacy Policy

kokofocus — Point and draw on your screen

Last updated: September 18, 2026

In brief: the developer collects no personal data. Preferences stay on your Mac. In version 1.1, optional Window Share processes a window you select locally, and Check for Updates contacts Apple only when you request it. Your meeting app handles sharing with participants.

Data we collect

None. The developer does not collect, request, or receive any personal information, identifiers, usage metrics, diagnostics, or content of any kind. There is no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, no advertising network, and no developer-operated server. kokofocus has no account system and nothing to sign in to.

Network activity

Version 1.0 makes no network connections. Starting in version 1.1, choosing Check for Updates makes an HTTPS request to Apple’s public app catalog using kokofocus’s App Store ID and the region configured on your Mac. It sends no screen content, annotations, preferences, account information, or device identifier. The request uses no cookies or persistent cache. Apple receives the network information needed to answer the request, including your IP address; Apple’s own privacy policy applies to its service.

Updates install through the Mac App Store. kokofocus does not run an update server or automatically check for updates in the background. Opening the App Store or a support link uses the destination service under its own privacy policy.

Data stored on your device

kokofocus stores a small set of preferences locally, inside the app’s sandboxed container managed by macOS:

  • Your selected tool, colour, laser size and stroke weight.
  • Your interaction settings: which key opens the tool ring, the hold duration, and whether the double-tap shortcut is enabled.
  • Display preferences such as whether shapes fade automatically and after how long.
  • Your tool shortcut assignments and tutorial display preference.

Annotations and optional Window Share frames are held in memory, not saved as recordings or documents. They are discarded when the session ends. Preferences remain in the app’s sandbox container; macOS may retain that container after uninstalling.

Window Share (beta), available in version 1.1

You choose the source through Apple’s system window picker. kokofocus captures that authorized source, combines its video with your annotations locally, and displays the result in a separate presentation window. It does not capture audio, save recordings, or upload the frames. Ending Window Share stops capture.

To show the result to others, you separately select kokofocus’s presentation window in your meeting app. That app handles transmission and any meeting recording, under its own settings and privacy policy.

Permissions requested

Display annotation requires no capture access. Optional Window Share uses the macOS picker to authorize the selected source for the session, without asking for blanket Screen Recording access. kokofocus does not request Accessibility, Input Monitoring, camera, microphone, location, contacts, or file access.

The app reads cursor position, modifier-key state, mouse-button state, and Escape state to operate its tools. It does not record your typing or install a global event tap. In version 1.1, assigned tool shortcuts are registered with macOS while kokofocus is on and interaction is active; they pause in Settings, Tutorial, and sharing setup. These combinations can take precedence over another app’s shortcuts. When you record a shortcut in Settings, only the assignment you choose is saved.

What your audience sees

Sharing a display includes the annotations drawn on it. Window Share includes the selected source and annotations in the presentation window. The tool ring and tool indicator are excluded from capture by default; a setting lets you include them. The developer receives none of this content.

Third-party services

Apple provides the system capture picker, app catalog, and Mac App Store. Version 1.1 also includes the open-source KeyboardShortcuts library for keyboard shortcut representations and labels; it adds no analytics or network service. Its MIT license is bundled with the app. There are no advertising or analytics SDKs.

Tracking

None. kokofocus does not track you across apps or websites and uses no advertising identifier.

Children’s privacy

kokofocus is rated 4+ and collects no data from any user, including children.

Your rights

The developer holds no personal data to export, correct, or delete. You can reset tool shortcuts within Settings. To remove all local preferences, quit kokofocus and remove its sandbox container at ~/Library/Containers/com.shutas.kokofocus. Uninstalling alone may leave preferences behind.
